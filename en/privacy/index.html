Privacy policy

This policy explains how Siwnin Productions ("we") handles personal information on this website (https://siwninproductions.com, "this site").

01Information we collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Information you give us when you contact us by email, such as your name, email address, company or organisation, and the content of your enquiry
  • Access information recorded automatically when you visit this site, such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and time of visit
  • A cookie that remembers your language setting

02How we use it

We use this information to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and contact you about events and proposals
  • Run and improve this site, and protect it from unauthorised access
  • Remember your settings, such as your preferred language

03Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

04Service providers

We may use outside providers to run parts of this site, such as hosting and email. When we do, we make sure they handle personal information properly.

05Cookies and analytics

This site uses a cookie to remember your language setting.

This site uses analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how it is used. These tools use cookies to collect data that does not identify you personally.

You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

06Keeping your data safe

We take appropriate measures to prevent the loss, misuse or leakage of the personal information we hold.

07Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, stop using or delete the personal information we hold about you. We will confirm your identity and respond promptly, in line with the law.
